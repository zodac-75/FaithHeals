FaithHeals

HealBot-style healing frames for WoW Forever. Clean party and raid bars, and a heal on every button of your mouse, built with 12-button MMO mice in mind.

Click-casting

  • Pick a spell from your spellbook, or Target, Set focus, Assist, Unit menu or Trade.
  • Press the mouse button or key you want, with Alt, Ctrl and Shift combinations.
  • "Max" always casts your highest rank, or pick a fixed rank.
  • Hover a bar to see what every button casts with the modifier you are holding.

Made for MMO mice

  • The thumb buttons and the mouse wheel cast on the bar under your cursor.
  • They only work over the bars, so they keep their normal action-bar job everywhere else.
  • Optional: keys cast on your current target when the mouse is not over a bar.

On the bars

  • Health %, incoming heals, class or health colours (green to red).
  • Icons with one toggle each: your HoTs, buffs and debuffs (all, or only the ones you can dispel), each with its own max and size.
  • Tanks on top of the frame, marked MT or OT (Main Tank / Main Assist from the raid leader, or the tank role), and a small icon on healers.
  • A border when you can dispel a debuff, a border on your target, raid markers, and fading for players out of range.

Look and layout

  • Four themes, plus font, text size, outline, shadow, name colour, background and fill direction.
  • Its own bar size for Solo, Party and Raid 10, 20, 25 and 40, switching by itself.
  • Columns or rows, sorted by raid group, role or name.
  • Test bars: /fh test 20 shows fake group members, so you can set it all up without a group.
  • Drag to move, drag the corner to resize, mouse wheel to scale.

Getting started

Type /fh to open the settings. It comes with a Restoration Druid setup, but you can put any healer's spells on any button.

Please report problems in the comments (/fh debug helps).
