FaithHeals

HealBot-style healing frames for WoW Forever. Clean party and raid bars, and a heal on every button of your mouse, built with 12-button MMO mice in mind.

Click-casting like HealBot's Spells tab

  • Put a spell on Left, Right, Middle, Button 4, Button 5 and the mouse wheel.
  • Seven combinations for each button: Click, Shift, Ctrl, Alt, Ctrl+Shift, Alt+Shift and Alt+Ctrl.
  • Instead of a spell you can use target, focus or menu.
  • Hover a bar to see what every button casts with the modifier you are holding.

Made for MMO mice

  • All twelve thumb buttons can cast on the bar under your cursor.
  • They only work over the bars, so they keep their normal action-bar job everywhere else.
  • Pick which key each thumb button sends in the settings: click the box and press the button.
  • Optional: thumb buttons cast on your current target when the mouse is not over a bar.

On the bars

  • Class colours or health colours (green to red), health %, and the names you choose.
  • Your own HoTs with timers, a border when someone has a debuff you can dispel, and a border on your target.
  • Players who are out of range fade.

Look and layout

  • Four themes: Classic HealBot, Class colours, Slim class and Dark. Change bar colour, name colour, texture and size afterwards.
  • Columns or rows, sorted by raid group, role or name, with your choice of bars per column and spacing.
  • Test bars: /fh test 20 shows fake group members, so you can set it all up without a group.
  • Drag to move, drag the corner to resize, mouse wheel to scale. Text and icons grow and shrink with the bars.

Getting started

Type /fh to open the settings. It comes with a Restoration Druid setup (Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Wild Growth, Lifebloom, Swiftmend, Nature's Cure, Ironbark and more), but you can put any healer's spells on any button.

This is an early version. Please report any Lua errors you get in the comments.
